title: VLK is ready for the app stores
date: 2026-10-09
tags: [mobile, release]
draft: false
---

This week VLK moved from "it works on my phone" to "ready to submit". Both the iOS and Android apps now build with proper signing for the stores, there is a privacy policy, and the app can ask people to update, either gently or, for old builds, as a requirement.

It is a quieter kind of milestone than a new feature, and it is the one that makes everything else reachable by real people.
