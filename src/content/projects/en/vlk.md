VLK is a mobile app for iOS and Android focused on forest protection.
It is currently in development. A detailed case study will follow once the app is published.

## Starting point

This is where the app started: its original state, rebuilt from the code. A map with a bottom sheet, a side menu, an area-info screen, a report form with PDF and DOCX export, and settings. It works, but it looks like a default Ionic app: blue toolbars and grey chrome, with no brand identity yet.

![The original state of the VLK app: map with bottom sheet, side menu, area info, report form and settings, in default Ionic styling](../../../assets/vlk/app-original-state.png)

The redesign explores how the VLK brand can live in the app's chrome while the content stays white, cream and easy to read in sunlight.
